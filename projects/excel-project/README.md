Sales Performance Analysis Using Microsoft Excel

Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance using Microsoft Excel to evaluate revenue, profitability, cost of goods sold (COGS), regional performance, product profitability, and sales representative contributions.

The dashboard provides an overview of business performance, with a reported profit margin of approximately 20%.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Revenue: ₦2,328,370,000 (₦2.33 billion)
* Total Cost of Goods Sold (COGS): ₦1,862,696,000 (₦1.86 billion)
* Total Profit: ₦465,674,000 (₦465.67 million)
* Total Customers Served: 2,098
* Profit Margin: Approximately 20%

Key Insights

1. Product Profitability

* Top Performer: Laptop A15 is the leading product in terms of profit contribution, highlighting its importance to overall profitability.
* Lower Profit Contributors: The Wall Clock and Blender B10 record the lowest profit contributions. Further analysis of their sales volumes, pricing, and costs could help explain their performance.

2. Regional Cost Analysis

* Higher-Cost Locations: Lagos and Port Harcourt record the highest COGS among the locations analyzed.
* Other Locations: Abuja and Ibadan have comparatively lower COGS.

These differences may reflect variations in sales volume, product mix, or operating costs. Further investigation would be needed to identify the underlying causes.

3. Sales Representative Performance

* Top Performer: Peter generates the highest revenue, exceeding ₦600 million.
* Other Contributors: Musa and Gbenga also demonstrate strong revenue performance.
* Opportunity for Improvement: Africa records lower revenue than the other representatives, presenting an opportunity to review sales performance and identify areas for support.

4. Sales Trends Over Time

The timeline chart shows fluctuations in revenue, with several peaks and declines during the period analyzed. Further investigation could help determine whether these patterns are associated with seasonality, promotions, or other business factors.

Recommendations

1. Optimize Product Mix: Review inventory and marketing strategies for high-profit products such as Laptop A15 while evaluating the performance of lower-profit products.
2. Investigate Regional Costs: Examine the factors contributing to higher COGS in Lagos and Port Harcourt to identify opportunities for cost optimization.
3. Improve Sales Performance: Review the approaches used by top-performing representatives and identify opportunities to support representatives with lower revenue.
4. Monitor Sales Trends: Track revenue over time to identify recurring patterns and support inventory and sales planning.

Tools and Skills Demonstrated

* Microsoft Excel
* Data cleaning and preparation
* KPI analysis
* Profitability and revenue analysis
* Regional performance analysis
* Sales trend analysis
* Dashboard design and data visualization
* Business insights and recommendations

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to transform sales data into meaningful business insights. The analysis highlights product profitability, regional cost differences, sales representative performance, and revenue trends to support data-informed business decisions.
